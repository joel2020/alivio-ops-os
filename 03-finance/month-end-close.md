# Month-End Close

**Owner:** Joel (Principal) · **Trigger:** 25th (prep), last business day (close) · **Cadence:** Monthly · **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-26

## Purpose

Close each month so the numbers are trustworthy, and so year-end is a formality
rather than an archaeology project. A month closed in ninety minutes is a month
that does not have to be reconstructed in March.

What breaks without it: uncategorised expenses accumulate, project profitability
is never known, and the annual tax preparation becomes a multi-day scramble
through bank statements.

## The 25th — prep, automated

Produces a list of gaps while there is still time to fix them:

- Uncategorised transactions
- Missing receipts over the threshold
- Invoices issued but unpaid, with ages
- Contractor invoices not yet received for the month
- Projects marked Delivered but not Closed — usually an unpaid final invoice
- Anything in the checklist below not yet done

## The close — last business day, ~90 minutes

### Revenue
- [ ] Every billing trigger that fired this month produced an invoice
- [ ] All invoices logged with issue date, due date, amount
- [ ] Payments received recorded and matched to invoices
- [ ] AR aging report generated
- [ ] Retainers invoiced for next month

### Costs
- [ ] Contractor invoices received and reconciled against Linear
- [ ] Payment run for the 1st prepared
- [ ] All expenses categorised — no "uncategorised" remaining
- [ ] Receipts attached for anything over $75
- [ ] Subscriptions reviewed — anything unused, cancelled

### Project profitability
- [ ] For each project delivered this month: revenue, contractor cost, gross margin
- [ ] Actual hours versus estimated
- [ ] Unbilled scope changes totalled

**This is the section that changes pricing.** Without it, Alivio knows revenue and
not whether it was worth doing — and the most damaging engagement is the profitable-
looking one that consumed 40% more hours than it was priced for.

### Cash and tax
- [ ] Bank reconciled
- [ ] 13-week model rebuilt from actuals
- [ ] Tax set-aside calculated and transferred if quarter-end

### Scorecard
- [ ] The 8–12 numbers in `../06-metrics/scorecard.md` updated
- [ ] Written note on any metric that moved badly

### Housekeeping
- [ ] Projects Delivered but not Closed chased
- [ ] Post-mortems complete for everything delivered
- [ ] Decision log current

## Decision rights

- **Joel decides alone:** all of it.
- **Escalate when:** n/a.

## Definition of done

Books reconciled, scorecard updated, no uncategorised transactions, and every
delivered project has a computed gross margin.

## Assumptions

- **No accounting tool is named in Alivio's stack.** Every step here assumes one
  exists. Until it does, this is a spreadsheet exercise and takes considerably
  longer than 90 minutes. **Choosing one is the highest-leverage unblock in the
  finance pillar.**
- The $75 receipt threshold is a common default, not a rule Joel has set.
- Assumes Joel does the close personally. It is the most delegable finance task
  once there is a bookkeeper, and the first one worth delegating.
