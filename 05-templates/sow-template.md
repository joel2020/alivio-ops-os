## 6. Revisions

**Two rounds of revisions are included per deliverable.** A round is one
consolidated set of feedback delivered at once.

- Clarifying questions are not a round
- Fixing something that does not match this SOW is not a round — that is finishing
  the work
- A change of direction is a round
- Round three onward is a change order, quoted as a fixed price before any work starts

## Decision rights

- **Joel decides alone:** price, terms, what is in and out of scope, portfolio
  rights, and whether to issue a SOW at all.
- **PM decides alone:** nothing. The PM may draft; Joel owns and signs.
- **Escalate when:** a client asks to amend sections 8, 9, or 11. Commercial and
  IP terms are never negotiated by anyone but Joel.

## Definition of done

Every `[BRACKET]` is replaced, every deliverable has a checkable acceptance
criterion, the exclusions list is specific to this engagement rather than
boilerplate, and a contractor who has never met the client could build from it.

## Assumptions

- **40/30/30, Net 7, two rounds, and the 2% late fee all come from
  `../03-finance/invoicing-policy.md` and `../01-delivery/scope-change-protocol.md`.**
  Change them there, not here, or the OS contradicts itself.
- **Alivio does not bill hourly** (Joel, 2026-07-27). Every number in this
  document is a fixed price for defined scope, including change orders. There is
  no hourly rate to quote and no variable invoice to reconcile.
- **This template has not been reviewed by a lawyer.** Sections 9, 11, and the
  delay clause are operational drafting, not legal advice.
