# Weekly Review Agenda

**Owner:** Joel (Principal) · **Trigger:** Friday · **Cadence:** Weekly · **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-26

## Purpose

The 30 minutes on Friday that keep the business honest — pipeline clean, cash
understood, nothing rotting quietly. Everything here is checked against a number,
not a feeling.

Total: **30 minutes.** If it runs longer, something has been neglected during the
week and that is the finding.

---

## Friday 15:30 — Pipeline, 15 min

*After the automated hygiene sweep has run.*

- [ ] **Every open deal has a next step with a date.** Any without → add one or
      mark Lost
- [ ] **Any deal past its stage dwell limit?** → advance or Lost
- [ ] **Any deal untouched 5+ business days?** → follow up per the cadence
- [ ] Missing required fields filled
- [ ] Won deals: deposit actually received? No deposit means Verbal, not Won
- [ ] **Recompute weighted pipeline. Write the number down.**
- [ ] Coverage against the 90-day target — above 3×?
- [ ] If coverage dropped two weeks running, write why

**Writing the number down is the point.** A forecast that is computed and not
recorded cannot show a trend, and the trend is the useful part.

## Friday 16:00 — Close-out, 15 min

*After the automated close-out has run.*

### Delivery
- [ ] Every active project has a stage that matches reality
- [ ] **Anything amber?** What is the plan
- [ ] **Anything red?** Is it in the daily check-in
- [ ] Any project aging past its stage dwell limit
- [ ] Every client got a status report on Wednesday

### Money
- [ ] **AR aging: anything crossed a chase tier this week?**
- [ ] **Anything over 30 days?** That is a RED trigger on its project
- [ ] Invoices that should have been issued this week and were not
- [ ] 13-week low point — which trigger level is Alivio in

### Week
- [ ] What shipped
- [ ] What slipped, and why
- [ ] What carries into next week
- [ ] Top 3 for next week — provisional, confirmed Monday

### Hygiene
- [ ] Decisions from this week logged
- [ ] Post-mortems done for anything that closed
- [ ] Anything that happened twice this week that should become an SOP

---

## The four questions

Ask them every week. They catch what checklists miss:

1. **What did I do this week that a contractor should have done?**
   The answer is the next thing to delegate or document.
2. **What am I avoiding?**
   Usually a client conversation, and usually getting more expensive.
3. **What would break first if I were away for two weeks?**
   That is the 90-day goal, measured weekly.
4. **What did I learn that is not written down anywhere?**

## Decision rights

- **Joel decides alone:** all of it.
- **Escalate when:** n/a.

## Definition of done

Pipeline clean, weighted number recorded, AR reviewed against tiers, next week's
top 3 provisionally set.

## Assumptions

- Assumes the Phase 3 automations have already run, so this is review rather than
  data gathering. **Without them it takes closer to an hour**, which is the
  practical argument for building Phase 3.
- Assumes Friday afternoon suits Joel. The sequence matters more than the time:
  hygiene before pipeline review, close-out last.
