# Kickoff Agenda

**Owner:** PM · **Trigger:** Project enters Kickoff · **Cadence:** Once per project · **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-26

## Purpose

The running order for the kickoff call. Process and reasoning are in
`../01-delivery/kickoff.md`; this is the thing you have open during the meeting.

Send to the client 24 hours ahead.

---

## Agenda — [PROJECT], [DATE], 45–60 min

**Attending:** [CLIENT NAMES] · [PM] · Joel (first 10 minutes) · [CONTRACTORS]

### 1. Introductions and handover — 10 min
*Joel present for this section only.*

- Who is who, and who does what
- **Joel, out loud:** "[PM] owns this from here. They'll be your day-to-day
  contact. I'm still on the account and you'll see me at milestones."

### 2. What we're building — 10 min

- Walk the deliverables list from the SOW
- Walk the **exclusions** list. Out loud. This is the cheapest moment to discover
  a mismatch
- Confirm acceptance criteria per deliverable

### 3. Success — 5 min

- **"What does success look like for you?"**
- **"What would make this a failure even if we delivered everything in the SOW?"**
- Write one sentence, agree it, read it back

### 4. How we'll work — 10 min

- Channel of record: [agreed]
- **Decision-maker: [NAME]** — confirm on the call
- Response times: ours 1 business day; theirs [N] business days per review round
- **Revisions: two rounds per deliverable.** What counts as a round
- Weekly status report, every Wednesday

### 5. Timeline and what we need from you — 15 min

- Walk milestones and checkpoint dates
- **Client inputs: what, who, by when** — enter into Linear live, on screen
- The delay clause, said once, warmly: *"If something on your side runs more than
  a week late, we'll need to move the dates — we'll flag it early rather than
  absorb it silently."*

### 6. Questions — 5 min

- **"Who else, besides you, could stop this from shipping?"**
- **"What's gone wrong for you with agencies before?"**
- Anything from them

### 7. Next steps — 5 min

- First checkpoint: [DATE]
- First thing we need: [INPUT] by [DATE]
- Recap in writing today

---

## After the call — PM, same day

- [ ] Written recap in the channel of record: decisions, owners, dates, the success
      sentence
- [ ] Notes to Obsidian; decisions to the decision log
- [ ] Client inputs in Linear with owners and dates
- [ ] Move to Build once every stage-2 exit criterion is met

**The recap goes out the same day.** A recap that arrives Thursday for a Monday
kickoff has already let two ambiguities harden.

## Decision rights

- **PM decides alone:** how the meeting runs, what is in the recap.
- **Escalate when:** the decision-maker is not who the SOW names, a promised input
  does not exist, or the client describes a different scope. All three are cheap
  now and expensive later.

## Definition of done

Recap sent same day, inputs in Linear with dates, and the client has said the PM's
name back to you.

## Assumptions

- Assumes a live call. Async kickoff works for small engagements, but the handover
  sentence in section 1 has to be synchronous or it does not land.
- Assumes contractors attend. For a one-person project the PM may run it alone.
