# Onboarding Path

**Owner:** Joel (Principal) · **Trigger:** A contractor is engaged · **Cadence:** Per person · **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-26

## Purpose

Get a new contractor productive on day one **without a synchronous walkthrough**.
That constraint is deliberate: an onboarding that requires Joel's live attention
does not scale past the first hire and fails entirely while Joel is away.

What breaks without it: every new contractor costs Joel half a day and learns a
slightly different version of how Alivio works.

## Before day 1 — Joel, 30 minutes

- [ ] Contractor agreement signed, IP assignment included
- [ ] W-9 / W-8BEN collected
- [ ] Rate agreed in writing
- [ ] Added to the contractor register
- [ ] Tool access provisioned:
  - [ ] Linear — the specific project only, not the workspace
  - [ ] GitHub — the specific repo
  - [ ] Obsidian / knowledge base — read access to the client folder
  - [ ] Channel of record — only if client-facing
- [ ] Sent: this OS, the project brief, and the SOW for their project

**Access is scoped to the project, not the company.** A contractor on the itslitneon
storefront has no reason to read RLTRS client notes, and default-narrow access
means offboarding is not a scramble.

## Day 1 — asynchronous, ~2 hours of their time

Sent as a single message, worked through in order:

1. **Read** `00-charter/operating-charter.md` — what Alivio does and refuses (10 min)
2. **Read** `00-charter/roles-and-decision-rights.md`, their role section — what they
   decide alone and what escalates (10 min)
3. **Read** the SOW for their project — what was actually promised (15 min)
4. **Read** `01-delivery/project-lifecycle.md` — stages and what "done" means (10 min)
5. **Read** `01-delivery/qa-gate.md` — the bar their work is measured against (10 min)
6. **Read** `01-delivery/client-communication-standard.md` — especially what they
   never say to a client (10 min)
7. **Do:** confirm tool access works; comment on their first Linear issue
8. **Do:** answer three questions back to the PM, in writing:
   - What is this project delivering, in your words?
   - What is explicitly out of scope?
   - What decision would you escalate rather than make?

**Step 8 is the actual test.** It takes ten minutes to read and reveals immediately
whether the documents worked. If the answers are wrong, the docs are wrong — fix
the doc, not just the person.

## Week 1

- [ ] First deliverable through the QA gate, however small — the point is
      exercising the gate early, not the deliverable
- [ ] PM checks in once, async, day 3
- [ ] Contractor has raised at least one question or friction point. **Zero
      questions in week one is a warning sign**, not a good sign
- [ ] Invoice format and submission deadline confirmed

## Day 30

- [ ] Joel and contractor, 30 minutes, live
- [ ] What is unclear that should not be?
- [ ] What in the OS is wrong, missing, or ignored in practice?
- [ ] Is the rate and scope still right for both sides?
- [ ] Continue, adjust, or end — decided explicitly, not by drift

**The day-30 conversation is a two-way review.** A contractor who has just learned
Alivio from cold is the best available auditor of these documents, and that
perspective is gone within a month.

## Offboarding a contractor

- [ ] Access revoked across every tool, same day
- [ ] Work committed, pushed, and handed over
- [ ] Final invoice received and paid on the next run
- [ ] Anything only they knew, written down before they go
- [ ] Register updated with an end date

## Decision rights

- **Joel decides alone:** who is engaged, rates, access, continuing past day 30.
- **PM decides alone:** day-to-day supervision, task assignment.
- **Escalate when:** a contractor is not going to work out. Early, not at day 60.

## Definition of done

A contractor completes day 1 without a live call, answers the three questions
correctly, and ships something through the QA gate in week 1.

## Assumptions

- Assumes contractors are experienced professionals needing context rather than
  training. Alivio's four archetypes are all senior roles; a junior hire needs a
  different path.
- **Assumes this OS is actually readable by someone outside Alivio.** Untested —
  the first contractor onboarded with it is the real test, and their confusion is
  data.
- Assumes async onboarding is acceptable. Some people need a call; offering one is
  fine, *requiring* one means the documents failed.
