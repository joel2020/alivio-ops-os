# Meeting Standards

**Owner:** Joel (Principal) · **Trigger:** Any recurring or client meeting · **Cadence:** Continuous · **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-26

## Purpose

Every meeting produces an artifact. A meeting whose output is "we discussed it" is
a meeting that will be repeated, and at Alivio's size meeting time is the scarcest
resource in the business.

What breaks without it: the calendar fills with recurring calls that survive on
inertia while the actual coordination happens in messages anyway.

## The rules

1. **Every meeting has an agenda, sent in advance.** No agenda, no meeting — and
   the person who called it either sends one or cancels.
2. **Every meeting produces a written artifact**, published the same day, in the
   channel of record.
3. **Every meeting has one owner** who runs it and writes the artifact.
4. **Default 30 minutes.** 60 only when the agenda justifies it.
5. **Recorded via Granola** where participants consent. **The transcript is raw
   material, not the artifact** — decisions still get promoted to the decision log
   by a human.
6. **No status meetings.** Status is written, in the weekly report and in Linear. A
   meeting to read out status converts everyone's time into one person's
   convenience.
7. **Anyone may leave a meeting** they are not contributing to. Stated openly so
   nobody has to be brave about it.

## The standing meetings

That is all of them. Anything else is ad-hoc and must justify itself.

| Meeting | When | Owner | Attendees | Artifact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Week planning** | Mon 9:00, 30m | Joel | Joel | Top 3 priorities in Linear |
| **Pipeline review** | Fri 15:30, 15m | Joel | Joel | Clean CRM, next step on every deal |
| **Client kickoff** | Per project, 45–60m | PM | PM, client, Joel (first 10m), contractors | Written recap, same day |
| **Client checkpoint** | Per milestone, 30m | PM | PM, client | Status report update |
| **Handoff session** | Per project, 30–45m | PM | PM, client | Recording + handoff doc |
| **Red project check-in** | Daily while red, 5m | PM | PM, Joel | Decision or explicit continuation |
| **Monthly scorecard** | Monthly, 30m | Joel | Joel, PM | Updated scorecard + notes |
| **Quarterly OS review** | Quarterly, 90m | Joel | Joel, PM | Updated docs, bumped review dates |
| **Contractor day 30** | Per person, 30m | Joel | Joel, contractor | Continue / adjust / end |

**There is no daily standup.** At 4–6 concurrent clients with a small bench it
costs more than it coordinates. The red project check-in is the only daily
meeting, and it exists precisely because red is exceptional.

## Client meetings specifically

- **Confirm 24 hours ahead.** Reduces no-shows more than anything else.
- **Agenda in the invite**, not a separate message.
- **Recap the same day**, always in writing, always in the channel of record.
- **Never end without naming the next action, its owner, and its date.** A client
  meeting ending in "we'll be in touch" is a meeting that has to happen again.

## The artifact standard

Every recap contains, at minimum:

- Decisions made
- Actions, each with an owner and a date
- Anything explicitly deferred, and until when
- Anything raised and not resolved

Four headings. Long enough to be useful, short enough to write in ten minutes
while it is fresh.

## Decision rights

- **Meeting owner decides alone:** agenda, who attends, whether it is needed.
- **Joel decides alone:** what is on the standing list.
- **Escalate when:** a standing meeting has been skipped twice — it is either wrong
  or unowned, and per the cadence calendar it gets fixed or deleted.

## Definition of done

Every meeting on the standing list has an owner, an agenda, and produces its
artifact the same day.

## Assumptions

- Assumes Granola is in use for recording and participants consent. **Recording
  without consent is a legal problem in several jurisdictions**, including
  two-party-consent US states.
- Assumes async-first working. If Alivio moves to a synchronous rhythm, several of
  these become inadequate.
- **Past ~8 people:** a weekly delivery sync across PMs becomes worth its cost.
  Not at current size.
