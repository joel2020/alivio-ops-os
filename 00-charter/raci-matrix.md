# RACI Matrix

**Owner:** Joel (Principal) · **Trigger:** A workflow gets added, or two people both think they own something · **Cadence:** Quarterly · **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-26

## Purpose

Names the single **A**ccountable person for every recurring workflow. The common
agency failure is not missing work, it is two people each assuming the other had
it — which is how an invoice ages 45 days with nobody having decided not to chase
it.

**R** = does the work · **A** = accountable, exactly one, never two · **C** =
consulted before · **I** = informed after.

## Decision rights

- **Joel decides alone:** every **A** assignment on this page.
- **Escalate when:** a workflow has no **A**, or has two. Both are defects and get
  fixed here rather than negotiated in the moment.

## Definition of done

Every row has exactly one **A**. Verified at each quarterly review.

## Assumptions

- **Sales concentrated entirely on Joel is the largest single risk here.** Every
  sales row is A/R Joel, so "Joel off for two weeks" means the pipeline stops for
  two weeks. Tolerable at 4–6 clients; it becomes the binding constraint on growth
  before contractor capacity does. Flagged rather than solved, because solving it
  means either hiring or Joel accepting a pipeline pause — a business decision,
  not a process one.
- Assumes PM is trusted to run chase tiers 1–2. If Joel wants all money contact,
  move those rows and accept the load.
- **Past ~8 people:** add a Delivery Lead column and move portfolio-level **A**
  there. Not now.
