# AI in U.S. Home Services — Case-Study Evidence Register

**Prepared for:** Build With Alivio  
**Research date:** 2026-08-06  
**Status:** Public-source research; not Alivio client proof

## How to use this register

Every measured result below comes from a technology vendor's customer story. The business is named and, in most cases, a customer executive or manager is quoted, but the vendor controls publication. Treat every number as **vendor-reported/customer-attributed**, not independently verified.

For public copy:

- Say “In a vendor-published customer story…” or “According to [vendor]'s customer story…”
- Link the source next to the claim.
- Never imply that Build With Alivio produced these results or that another client will obtain the same result.
- Do not use customer or vendor logos without permission.
- Prefer the operational mechanism and measurement method over a spectacular headline.
- Do not repeat a revenue, ROI, or savings number without its timeframe and attribution caveat.

## Measured case studies

### 1. Peaden — after-hours and overflow booking

- **Business / trade:** Peaden, Florida; HVAC, plumbing, and electrical.
- **AI workflow:** Hatch Voice AI answers after-hours and overflow calls, qualifies requests, books into ServiceTitan, and can dispatch a technician. Hatch also handles Google LSA/Angi speed-to-lead, estimate follow-up, aged-lead nurture, member recontact, and seasonal outbound campaigns.
- **Implementation detail:** The AI covers call-center overflow and after-hours first, connects to ServiceTitan for booking/dispatch, and processes about 3,600 inbound calls per month. The story says Peaden reviews AI call transcripts to surface lost opportunities for human inside-sales follow-up.
- **Exact reported outcome:** Hatch reports a **nearly 3x conversion rate on after-hours and overflow calls “in just a matter of months.”** It also reports that Hatch influenced **30% of all revenue**, with an **82% Google LSA response rate** and **73% Angi response rate**.
- **Measurement window / baseline:** “A matter of months”; original and final conversion percentages are not disclosed. “Revenue influenced” is not defined as incremental revenue.
- **Source / date:** [Hatch customer story: Peaden](https://www.usehatchapp.com/case-studies/peaden-voice). Publication date not shown; accessed 2026-08-06.
- **Evidence classification:** Vendor-reported/customer-attributed measured case study.
- **Caveats / does not prove:** No raw data, control group, exact baseline, software cost, gross margin, or attribution method is supplied. Multiple Hatch workflows were deployed, so the 30% influenced-revenue figure cannot be assigned only to after-hours Voice AI.
- **Build With Alivio relevance:** Strong illustration of the “missed calls → immediate qualification → booked job” ROI chain. A client implementation should baseline after-hours call count, answer rate, booking rate, average gross profit per booked job, and AI/human escalation rate before launch.

### 2. Preferred Home Services — conversation intelligence and sales coaching

- **Business / trade:** Preferred Home Services; 24/7 residential plumbing, HVAC, and electrical.
- **AI workflow:** Rilla records and analyzes in-home sales conversations, scores key talking points, lets managers review condensed calls, and gives representatives access to examples from experienced teammates.
- **Implementation detail:** The customer story says one call previously required **2–3 hours** of manager review; Rilla reduced review to **20–30 minutes**, supporting more frequent coaching across comfort consultants.
- **Exact reported outcome:** Average sale increased by **more than $2,000 year over year**. Rilla reports TGL close rates improved **18%** and marketing-lead close rates improved **20%**. One new consultant moved from a **50% to 71% close rate in two months**.
- **Measurement window / baseline:** Average sale: year over year. New consultant: two months. The source does not give original/final company-level close rates or clarify whether the 18% and 20% figures are relative changes or percentage-point changes.
- **Source / date:** [Rilla customer story: Preferred Home Services](https://www.rilla.com/customer-stories/prefered-home-services). Publication date not shown; accessed 2026-08-06.
- **Evidence classification:** Vendor-reported/customer-attributed measured case study.
- **Caveats / does not prove:** No sample size, seasonality adjustment, control group, margin data, or breakdown of concurrent sales-process changes. It does not establish that AI alone caused the revenue change.
- **Build With Alivio relevance:** Strong support for a coaching implementation measured through review time, script adherence, close rate by lead source, average ticket, and ramp time for new salespeople.

### 3. Air Design — membership reactivation and seasonal scheduling

- **Business / trade:** Air Design, Salt Lake area; residential and commercial home services, 75 employees and 55 trucks according to the story.
- **AI workflow:** Podium's AI Membership Coordinator identifies customers due for seasonal service, sends text outreach, checks the dispatch board, and books directly into the calendar.
- **Implementation detail:** The prior process involved manually working through about 600 paper membership folders and calling customers for **3–4 hours per day** during spring and fall.
- **Exact reported outcome:** In the **first six weeks**, the AI contacted **471 members**, booked **187 jobs**, and collected **$35,000 in revenue**, with no manual outreach calls by the office manager. The story reports **3–4 hours per day** saved during that workflow.
- **Measurement window / baseline:** Six weeks. The prior manual workload is described, but prior-period booking and revenue outcomes are not supplied.
- **Source / date:** [Podium customer story: Air Design](https://homeservices.podium.com/resources/case-study/air-design-ai-membership-coordinator). Publication date not shown; accessed 2026-08-06.
- **Evidence classification:** Vendor-reported/customer-attributed measured case study.
- **Caveats / does not prove:** “Revenue collected” is not incremental revenue, gross profit, or cash net of discounts and software cost. No holdout group, prior-season conversion rate, cancellation rate, or capacity cost is provided.
- **Build With Alivio relevance:** Excellent example of extracting near-term ROI from an existing customer list. A responsible offer would start with overdue/seasonal service records, consent status, available capacity, and revenue plus gross-margin tracking by campaign.

### 4. Harley's Heating & Air Conditioning — scheduling, dispatch, and capacity

- **Business / trade:** Harley's Heating & Air Conditioning, Lincoln, Nebraska; 13-person HVAC shop.
- **AI workflow:** ServiceTitan Max combines an AI virtual agent, online scheduling, marketing automation, and an AI dispatch agent that handles routing and prioritization.
- **Implementation detail:** A pre-opening caller was booked by the virtual agent, assigned by the dispatch agent, moved to the first job, and had a technician onsite by 8:30 a.m. The shop replaced four rigid daily slots with two four-hour arrival windows and automated prioritization. A membership campaign also fed calls and online bookings into the workflow.
- **Exact reported outcome:** Technicians increased from **four calls per day to six or seven**, without added overtime. A campaign to a **600–700 member** base booked **250 appointments in three days**. The story also says the business was on pace for $3 million, but that broader revenue trajectory should not be attributed solely to the AI workflow.
- **Measurement window / baseline:** Calls per day is a before/after operating comparison; exact observation period is not stated. Membership bookings occurred over three days.
- **Source / date:** [ServiceTitan success story: Harley's Heating & Air](https://www.servicetitan.com/blog/success-story-max-harleys-heating-air), published 2026-06-25.
- **Evidence classification:** Vendor-reported/customer-attributed measured case study.
- **Caveats / does not prove:** This is a bundled operational transformation, not an isolated AI experiment. It does not report drive time, callback rate, customer satisfaction, margin per call, software cost, or how many of the 250 appointments came through the virtual agent versus online scheduling.
- **Build With Alivio relevance:** Strong model for pairing AI with real dispatch capacity rather than merely collecting messages. Measure calls per tech-day, overtime, drive time, schedule utilization, booking-to-completion, and gross profit per route.

### 5. Crown Roofing and Waterproofing — roofing lead response and follow-up

- **Business / trade:** Crown Roofing and Waterproofing, Florida; commercial and residential roofing.
- **AI workflow:** Hatch Voice AI replaces an after-hours call center, captures caller information, and emails transcripts/summaries to the customer-service team. Salesforce triggers immediate SMS, email, phone, and voicemail-drop outreach; AI continues estimate follow-up, canceled-appointment/aged-estimate nurture, reviews, referrals, and cross-sell conversations.
- **Implementation detail:** Non-phone leads sync from Salesforce to Hatch and receive multi-channel outreach “within seconds.” Voice calls produce a transcript and summary for next-morning review. The AI agent continues the conversation and escalates when needed.
- **Exact reported outcome:** The company attributed **$365,000 in sales to Hatch in 60 days** and reported **less than 1% opt-out**. The story quotes the operator saying reporting showed “100X ROI,” but does not publish the calculation.
- **Measurement window / baseline:** 60 days for attributed sales; 90 days for a separate activity count cited in the story. No pre-Hatch sales baseline or comparable 60-day period is provided.
- **Source / date:** [Hatch customer story: Crown Roofing](https://www.usehatchapp.com/case-studies/crown-roofing-and-waterproofing). Publication date not shown; accessed 2026-08-06.
- **Evidence classification:** Vendor-reported/customer-attributed measured case study.
- **Caveats / does not prove:** “Sales influenced/attributed” is not defined and may include revenue that would have closed without Hatch. No margin, cancellations, cost allocation, attribution window, or control group is shown. Do not publish the 100X ROI figure without methodology.
- **Build With Alivio relevance:** Useful roofing-specific example for speed-to-lead plus persistent estimate follow-up. An Alivio implementation should define influenced versus incremental revenue, source every closed job, track opt-outs, and report gross profit rather than top-line sales alone.

### 6. Warner Super Service — replacing after-hours answering costs

- **Business / trade:** Warner Super Service; plumbing and HVAC.
- **AI workflow:** Podium's voice AI answers after-hours and overflow calls, captures lead details, and supports automated booking confirmations, texts, and customer updates.
- **Implementation detail:** Warner replaced a third-party answering service that charged by the minute with 24/7 AI coverage.
- **Exact reported outcome:** The prior answering-service bills were **$800–$1,500 per month**; the story presents this as the monthly cost eliminated. It also reports business volume maintained with fewer technicians and a Google rating change from **4.1 to 4.7**, but neither is cleanly attributable to voice AI alone.
- **Measurement window / baseline:** Monthly answering-service invoices. Exact post-implementation period and the AI subscription cost are not disclosed.
- **Source / date:** [Podium customer story: Warner Super Service](https://homeservices.podium.com/resources/case-study/warner-super-service). Publication date not shown; accessed 2026-08-06.
- **Evidence classification:** Vendor-reported/customer-attributed measured cost case.
- **Caveats / does not prove:** Eliminated vendor spend is not net savings until AI software, setup, oversight, escalations, and failure handling are included. The review rating change covers broader Podium use, not only AI. The reduced technician count is not explained and should not be used as an AI labor claim.
- **Build With Alivio relevance:** Useful for a conservative “cost removed” ROI model. Compare current answering-service/overflow cost, missed-call volume, net AI cost, qualified booking rate, and recovered gross profit.

### 7. Sloan Electric — solo-contractor response and office automation

- **Business / trade:** Sloan Electric; solo electrical contractor.
- **AI workflow:** Podium's AI Employee sends immediate preliminary responses while a connected platform centralizes phone, scheduling, estimates, invoicing, price book, transcripts, and automated review requests.
- **Implementation detail:** The AI sends a first message to each lead in **1–2 minutes**. Pricing data in the price book supports faster estimate creation, and call/text records remain attached to the customer workflow.
- **Exact reported outcome:** Owner Mark Sloan reports saving **10 hours per week**. Estimates that previously could take more than 24 hours were sent in about **10 minutes** after the new workflow. The source reports **1–2 minute** AI lead-response time.
- **Measurement window / baseline:** Per-week time estimate; individual estimate turnaround comparison. No study window or lead conversion result is disclosed.
- **Source / date:** [Podium customer story: Sloan Electric](https://homeservices.podium.com/resources/case-study/sloan-electric). Publication date not shown; accessed 2026-08-06.
- **Evidence classification:** Vendor-reported/customer-attributed measured case study.
- **Caveats / does not prove:** The 10-hour saving and estimate turnaround combine AI with a price book, invoicing, scheduling, and centralization. It does not show booking lift, revenue lift, error rate, or net value after subscription cost.
- **Build With Alivio relevance:** A relatable small-business example. Sell the workflow around protected billable hours and faster response, then measure admin minutes per estimate, response time, quotes sent, quote-to-job rate, and recovered field capacity.

### 8. High Ground Service Pros — phased voice-AI rollout

- **Business / trade:** High Ground Service Pros, New Jersey/New Hampshire/Maine; multi-trade HVAC, plumbing, and electrical, about 150 employees according to the story.
- **AI workflow:** Hatch Voice AI answers and books into ServiceTitan, escalates with live context, and shares a maintained knowledge base. Outbound SMS/email supports speed-to-lead, estimate follow-up, nurture, recontact, and seasonal campaigns.
- **Implementation detail:** Phase 1 routed calls unanswered after **15–20 seconds**, plus nights/weekends, to AI instead of voicemail. Staff reviewed calls and updated the knowledge base. Phase 2 expanded frontline coverage from one to two to five days per week; High Ground created a dedicated “Hatch AI” employee in ServiceTitan for reporting.
- **Exact reported outcome:** Abandoned-call rate fell from about **10% to 2%**. AI handled **75% of inbound call volume** and booked **81% of bookable leads**. The story says **123 leads** were recovered in the previous month, estimates **$250,000–$300,000 annual hiring-cost avoidance**, and reports **$100,000–$250,000 per month** from outbound SMS.
- **Measurement window / baseline:** One month for 123 recovered leads; annual estimate for cost avoidance; monthly outbound revenue; exact AI booking-rate observation period not stated.
- **Source / date:** [Hatch customer story: High Ground Service Pros](https://www.usehatchapp.com/case-studies/high-ground-service-pros). Publication date not shown; accessed 2026-08-06.
- **Evidence classification:** Vendor-reported/customer-attributed measured case study.
- **Caveats / does not prove:** Large, multi-location operator; results may not transfer to an SMB. “Recovered” assumes those leads would otherwise be lost. Cost avoidance is an estimate, outbound revenue may not be incremental, and no margin/software cost/control data is provided.
- **Build With Alivio relevance:** Best implementation blueprint in the set: start with calls already being missed, monitor every outcome, refine the knowledge base, add escalation summaries, and only then expand coverage. The public site can feature the phased method without leading with the enterprise-scale revenue numbers.

## Public-site claim pattern

Use wording such as:

> **What the workflow can look like:** In a Hatch-published customer story, Peaden said its AI answered and booked overflow and after-hours calls through a ServiceTitan-connected workflow. Hatch reports that Peaden nearly tripled conversion on those calls over a period of months. This is a third-party, vendor-reported result—not an Alivio client result or a guarantee. [Read the source.](https://www.usehatchapp.com/case-studies/peaden-voice)

Then transition to Alivio's method:

1. Baseline the client's current missed-call, response-time, booking, gross-profit, and labor metrics.
2. Deploy one bounded workflow with human escalation and audit logs.
3. Tag every AI-handled lead and job in the client's system of record.
4. Compare against the pre-launch baseline and, where practical, a holdout period or channel.
5. Report booked revenue, completed revenue, gross profit, software/implementation cost, and net ROI separately.

## Misclassification traps to avoid

- **TurnPoint's “58x more appointments” Hatch story is not measured AI evidence.** The published implementation used automated multi-touch email/text campaigns; the page describes AI agents as a future expansion. It can support an automation use case, but not an AI outcome claim.
- **Older Hatch rehash stories may describe rules-based campaign automation rather than conversational AI.** Only call them AI case studies when the source explicitly identifies the measured workflow as AI.
- **Product-page aggregates are not named-customer outcomes.** For example, Jobber's general AI Receptionist usage statistic should not be attached to a particular contractor or used as proof of ROI.

## Research limitations

- No independent, controlled evaluation of a named U.S. home-service operator's AI implementation was found in this research pass.
- Vendor customer stories are useful implementation evidence but weak causal evidence.
- Publication dates were absent on several case-study pages; the access date is recorded instead.
- The public website should never blend these third-party examples with Alivio-owned proof. A distinct label such as **“Industry examples (vendor-reported)”** is required.
